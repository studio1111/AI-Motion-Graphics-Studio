## Initial hypotheses to test
1. Hybrid 2D/3D and tactile treatments add specificity when they suit the subject.
2. Kinetic typography works best when motion follows linguistic emphasis and leaves reading holds.
3. Data animation is strongest when motion reveals a sourced relationship.
4. A small semantic sound vocabulary can outperform an effect on every cut.
5. One lead style with controlled accents improves coherence.
These are hypotheses until validated across varied L2/L3 observations.
