## Evidence levels
- L0: search result, thumbnail, title or metadata.
- L1: description, article, transcript or captions reviewed.
- L2: complete video watched.
- L3: timestamped scene breakdown with selected frames.

Only L2/L3 count as complete visual review. No item above claims L2 or L3. Keep metadata/video counts separate.

## Initial hypotheses (not universal rules)
1. Hybrid 2D/3D and tactile treatments can increase specificity when relevant to the topic.
2. Kinetic type is stronger when motion follows linguistic emphasis and allows a reading hold.
3. Data graphics work best when progressive motion reveals an actual relationship.
4. A small semantic sound vocabulary can be more intentional than sound effects on every cut.
5. One lead style plus controlled accents supports coherence while allowing variation.
Validate these through a broader and more diverse sample before promoting them to general rules.
