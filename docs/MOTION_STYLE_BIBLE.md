# Motion Style Bible

Choose one lead visual language and at most two secondary influences. Treat styles as systems of composition, typography, color, materials, motion, pacing, and sound, not as filters. These are original recipes, not shot-by-shot copies of another creator's work.

## 01. Editorial kinetic typography
**Best for:** arguments, social issues, essays, high-impact claims.  
**Typography:** oversized condensed or expressive grotesk display; neutral text face; optional mono evidence labels.  
**Color and layout:** paper/ink or high-contrast editorial base, one sharp signal accent, asymmetric grid, deliberate crop and negative space.  
**Motion:** phrase/word stagger following sentence stress, mask reveal, tracking compression, line wipes, one meaningful type-to-shape morph, controlled overshoot and readable holds.  
**Sound and pace:** intimate voice, tactile hits, restrained pulse, silence before a reveal; sharp but not relentless.  
**Avoid:** constant jitter, random glitch, unreadable cropping, identical character-by-character karaoke.

## 02. Data journalism and infographics
**Best for:** economics, public policy, engineering, comparisons, statistics.  
**Typography:** legible sans labels, tabular numerals or mono measurements, oversized key values.  
**Color/layout:** restrained neutral canvas, consistent semantic colors, visible units, source notes, chart-first hierarchy. Color must not be the only encoding.  
**Motion:** bars grow from correct baselines, lines trace routes, annotations attach to data, aligned comparisons change progressively.  
**Sound/pace:** subtle ticks, low-detail bed, slower holds for comparison.  
**Avoid:** misleading axes, unlabeled units, false precision, floating numbers without context.

## 03. Scientific 2D and 2.5D
**Best for:** physics, biology, mechanisms, engineering, abstract processes.  
**Typography:** geometric or humanist sans, short labels and directional annotations.  
**Color/layout:** stable functional color roles, layered cutaways, exploded views, transparent planes, clear foreground/midground/background.  
**Motion:** trace only the relevant path, assemble/disassemble, reveal a cross-section, orbit a mechanism, then return to context; use parallax only when it clarifies depth.  
**Sound/pace:** precise ticks and quiet mechanical Foley; structure as cause → process → effect.  
**Avoid:** inaccurate mechanisms, particles presented as evidence, fake depth obscuring the mechanism.

## 04. Cinematic documentary and editorial titles
**Best for:** history, investigative themes, biographies, technology and long-form openings.  
**Typography:** disciplined editorial sans with optional serif contrast when culturally appropriate; labels serve information.  
**Color/layout:** limited dark/light base, one motivated signal color, layered frames and meaningful negative space.  
**Motion:** revealing close detail, slow deliberate push, map or artifact match cut, depth parallax, one decisive reveal per major beat.  
**Sound/pace:** atmosphere and low-end used sparingly; simmer, reveal, consequence, then breathing room.  
**Avoid:** generic black-and-gold “prestige”, meaningless grain, repeated lens flares, all-scenes-as-title-cards.

## 05. Tactile and archival collage
**Best for:** culture, memory, identity, history and documentary evidence.  
**Typography:** editorial display plus mono annotations; handwriting is a rare accent.  
**Color/layout:** paper/ink foundation, tactile photographic fragments, scanned textures and one contemporary accent.  
**Motion:** paper-plane parallax, cutout pivots, mask reveals, controlled registration offsets and visible assembly.  
**Sound/pace:** restrained paper, mechanical and room-tone textures; slightly irregular rhythm with precise timing.  
**Avoid:** fake archive claims, fabricated documents, excessive noise, unreadable distressed fonts.

## 06. Premium 3D and technology/product films
**Best for:** devices, industrial mechanisms, future-facing systems, product reveals.  
**Typography:** clean display sans plus short technical labels, composited separately.  
**Color/layout:** materials and reflections tied to the actual subject; hero object, macro detail and clean negative space.  
**Motion:** macro-to-wide reveal, physically plausible orbit, part separation, precise speed ramp, controlled light sweep, stable end pose.  
**Sound/pace:** material-specific Foley, subtle servo/mechanical detail, few deliberate shots with hero holds.  
**Avoid:** unstable geometry, melted lettering, impossible reflections, random spins, fabricated product claims.

## 07. Character-led 2D and shape animation
**Best for:** approachable education, human stories, ideas explained through metaphor.  
**Typography:** friendly, legible type pairing; separate text from character action.  
**Color/layout:** expressive but limited palette, readable silhouettes, stable prop and rig language.  
**Motion:** anticipation, clear pose changes, believable follow-through, controlled squash/stretch and secondary motion that conveys weight.  
**Sound/pace:** gesture-linked Foley, light tonal accents, dialogue space.  
**Avoid:** stock-looking characters, inconsistent proportions, stiff puppets, comedy that undermines serious facts.

## 08. Swiss and modernist information design
**Best for:** process explainers, structured lessons, high-clarity educational systems.  
**Typography:** neo-grotesk or humanist sans, compact labels and stable numeric formatting.  
**Color/layout:** strict grid, precise alignment, controlled asymmetry, modular connections and a small semantic palette.  
**Motion:** grid reconfiguration, modules connecting, line-traced relationships, alignment snaps and spatially coherent transitions.  
**Sound/pace:** quiet and precise, with changes that map to meaning.  
**Avoid:** lifeless slide-deck motion, repeated rounded cards, rigidly identical scenes.

## 09. Generative and procedural abstraction
**Best for:** networks, mathematics, music, systems and emergence.  
**Typography:** sparse, highly legible labels and focal annotations.  
**Color/layout:** coherent procedural palette, stable focal anchors, controlled density.  
**Motion:** seeded deterministic particles, vector fields, parametric paths and phases that build complexity before simplifying into insight.  
**Sound/pace:** synthesized pulse and granular texture tied to actual state changes.  
**Avoid:** beautiful but meaningless noise, unseeded randomness, strobe-like changes, unreproducible visuals.

## 10. Maps, timelines and historical visualization
**Best for:** journeys, migration, geopolitics, chronology and historical cause-and-effect.  
**Typography:** legible place names, mono/tabular dates, a consistent hierarchy.  
**Color/layout:** persistent geographic/timeline anchor, sourced boundaries, stable routes and explicit uncertainty where appropriate.  
**Motion:** route drawing, timeline scrubbing, orientation-aware camera travel, map-to-object match cuts and event stacking.  
**Sound/pace:** restrained movement cues and clear narration; allow labels to be read.  
**Avoid:** unsourced borders, anachronistic maps, unlabeled dates, motion that implies unsupported causality.

## 11. Vertical and social-native explainers
**Best for:** short-form 9:16 mobile stories.  
**Typography:** robust display face, concise lines, captions that survive muted viewing.  
**Color/layout:** one dominant idea per frame, strong foreground/background separation, platform UI safe zones.  
**Motion:** immediate first-beat hook, dynamic crops, fast but controlled reveals and short readable holds.  
**Sound/pace:** voice clarity first, small signature motif, purposeful speed ramps.  
**Avoid:** tiny detail, rushed captions, UI collisions, random zooms mistaken for energy.

## 12. Live action plus graphic annotation
**Best for:** journalism, field science, interviews and real-world product/process explainers.  
**Typography:** editorial hierarchy that respects footage exposure and focus.  
**Color/layout:** footage anchors credibility; overlays use tested contrast and restrained semantic colors.  
**Motion:** tracked callouts, masks around subjects, freeze/annotation moments and match cuts to simplified diagrams.  
**Sound/pace:** preserve room tone and relevant original context; let footage breathe before annotating.  
**Avoid:** untracked overlays, invented measurements, hiding key action or altering factual context.

## Style selection protocol
Score candidates 0–5 for topic fit, audience clarity, emotional fit, distinctiveness, asset feasibility and implementation cost. Pick the highest-fit lead style, add no more than two accents, and record why. Industry trend reports can seed hypotheses, but a style only becomes an evidence-backed “rule” after varied references support it. See `research/REFERENCE_SEED_CATALOG.md`.
