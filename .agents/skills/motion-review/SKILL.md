# Motion Design Review

Review the brief, project files, preview, and final render. Do not claim to have viewed or rendered media that is unavailable.

## Review passes
1. Story: is the message clear and each beat necessary?
2. Composition: focal hierarchy, alignment, negative space, safe areas, contrast.
3. Motion: easing, anticipation, overshoot, continuity, pacing, and motion purpose.
4. Typography: copy accuracy, line breaks, legibility, glyph coverage, and overflow.
5. Cinematography: camera motivation, spatial continuity, lighting consistency.
6. Audio: dialogue intelligibility, sync, clipping, and mix balance.
7. Technical: frame rate, dimensions, duration, codec, black/frozen frames, flicker, and audio sync.
8. Delivery: filenames, source files, licenses, credits, and reproducible settings.

Return findings by severity, with file/shot/timecode and a concrete fix. Re-run relevant checks after changes.
