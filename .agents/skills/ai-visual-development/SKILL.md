# AI Visual Development

## Before generation
- Define subject, setting, art direction, composition, lens/camera, lighting, palette, texture, and aspect ratio.
- Separate required content from style descriptors and optional details.
- Maintain a continuity sheet for recurring subjects, wardrobe, props, locations, and colors.
- Generate or collect keyframes for the beginning, middle, and end of important movements.
- Plan how generated footage will cut together and where typography/graphics will be composited.

## Prompt structure
Subject and action; environment; composition; camera and lens behavior; lighting; visual style; motion; constraints; aspect ratio.
Avoid conflicting camera instructions and excessive adjective lists.

## Quality control
Check anatomy, object permanence, text artifacts, temporal consistency, flicker, unintended logos, and unwanted changes between frames. Prefer clean plates or separate graphic layers when exact typography is required. Document the model/tool and generation settings used.
