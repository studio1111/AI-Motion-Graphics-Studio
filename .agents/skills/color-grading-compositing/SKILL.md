# Color Grading and Compositing

1. Confirm the source color spaces, working space, display transform, output color metadata, and delivery platform.
2. Establish exposure, contrast, and white balance before creative grading.
3. Separate correction from look development; preserve highlight and shadow detail.
4. Use masks, mattes, keying, and glow to support focal hierarchy, not hide weak composition.
5. Match generated footage and 3D renders before applying a shared look.
6. Keep grain, halation, bloom, lens distortion, and chromatic aberration consistent and restrained.
7. Test gradients, banding, alpha edges, skin tones when relevant, and dark-scene detail after encoding.
8. Document LUTs, plugins, color transforms, and their licenses.

Never claim color accuracy without inspecting the actual rendered output and metadata.
