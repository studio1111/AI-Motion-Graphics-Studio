# Logo and Brand Animation

1. Inspect supplied logo assets and brand rules. Prefer vector source when available.
2. Preserve the logo's geometry, proportions, clear space, colors, and minimum size.
3. Choose a reveal concept connected to the brand meaning, not a generic effect.
4. Plan silhouette, assembly/reveal, settle, hold, and optional end-card timing.
5. Keep particles, distortion, extrusion, and lighting from obscuring brand recognition.
6. Test on light, dark, and transparent backgrounds when required.
7. Export the requested aspect ratios and alpha channel only when the target format supports it.
8. Provide a static fallback and avoid altering trademarks without permission.

Deliver a storyboard, timing, background variants, export settings, and source project notes.
