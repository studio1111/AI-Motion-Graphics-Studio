# Subtitles and Accessibility

- Preserve the transcript; distinguish verbatim captions from edited subtitles.
- Verify timestamps, reading speed, line breaks, speaker labels, and sound descriptions where relevant.
- Keep captions inside safe areas and away from important on-screen action.
- Test font glyph coverage, shaping, punctuation, and RTL/LTR mixing for each language.
- Maintain sufficient contrast and readable size at actual mobile playback size.
- Avoid conveying meaning through color or motion alone.
- Provide a non-flashing alternative; avoid rapid flashing and respect reduced-motion needs for interactive experiences.
- Check that burned-in text and selectable subtitle tracks are not accidentally both enabled.
