# Audio and Sound Design

- Establish whether the piece is voice-led, music-led, or sound-design-led.
- Build a cue sheet mapped to shot/beat timings.
- Use sound to reinforce meaningful motion, not to punctuate every element.
- Preserve speech intelligibility; duck music under dialogue when appropriate.
- Avoid clipping and sudden loudness jumps; inspect true peaks and integrated loudness against the delivery target.
- Use only assets whose licenses permit the intended distribution and keep attribution records.
- Sync important visual hits to audio using frame/time calculations, not visual guesswork.
- Check the mix on headphones and a small speaker, and test the final encoded file.

Do not synthesize or imitate a real person's voice without clear authorization. Document voice, music, and SFX provenance.
