# Story-Driven Educational Documentary

## Narrative blueprint
1. Cold open with a striking consequence, contradiction, scale, or unanswered question.
2. Promise what the viewer will understand.
3. Establish context visually rather than with a long introduction.
4. Explain the mechanism using spatial diagrams, physical metaphors, annotated objects, maps, timelines, and cause-effect chains.
5. Escalate with a complication, constraint, counterexample, or scale shift.
6. Reveal the central insight in a visually unmistakable way.
7. Explain real-world implications and limitations.
8. Resolve the opening question and leave one memorable takeaway.

## Research discipline
Maintain a claim ledger: claim ID, script sentence, source URL/title, publication date, primary or secondary source, and uncertainty. Prefer original research, official datasets, primary documentation, and direct evidence. Use independent corroboration for consequential claims. Label illustrative reconstructions; generated images are not documentary evidence.

## Visual translation
For abstract concepts, consider at least three visual treatments before choosing: a physical analogy or miniature world; a precise diagram or animated model; real-world imagery with annotations. Select for comprehension, not spectacle alone.

## Scene cards
Each scene defines scene ID, duration, narration, purpose, metaphor, composition, depth layers, object states, camera path, motion curve, exact on-screen copy, data and source IDs, transitions, sound cues, assets, fallback, and acceptance checks.

## Retention with integrity
Use curiosity gaps that are resolved, progressive disclosure, recurring motifs, scale contrast, callbacks, and purposeful pattern interrupts. Avoid false urgency and misleading claims. Vary scene length based on beats, not equal-duration templates. Never rush narration to fit an arbitrary runtime.

## Accessibility
Keep labels readable, contrast strong, and margins safe. Do not encode meaning by color alone. For Persian and Arabic, verify RTL layout, punctuation, shaping, line breaks, and mixed Latin terms in the actual render.
## Status
Original workflow guide. External research services are not implied to be installed.
