# Cinematic Storyboarding

## Required brief
Audience, objective, platform, duration, aspect ratio, message, brand constraints, available assets, narration/music, and delivery format.

## Produce
- Logline and visual concept
- Beat sheet with approximate timings
- Shot list: framing, subject/action, camera, lighting, palette, transition, sound cue
- Keyframe prompts and continuity notes
- Typography and graphic overlay plan
- Asset list and risk/unknowns

## Editorial rhythm
Start with a readable hook. Alternate information density and visual breathing room. Make the main reveal clear, allow it to land, and finish with a deliberate resolution or CTA. Use cuts for energy, longer moves for discovery, and pauses for comprehension.

Do not promise a specific generated-video result; clearly separate creative intent from tool limitations.
