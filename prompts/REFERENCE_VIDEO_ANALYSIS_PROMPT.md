# Reference Video Craft Analysis Prompt

Analyze a public motion-graphics video as a production researcher, not merely by summarizing its topic.

## Inspect
- Hook at the beginning and how the promise is delivered
- Narrative beats, reveals, tension, explanation, and ending
- Timestamped scene breakdown and approximate duration distribution
- Visual modes: typography, vector, data, maps, 2.5D, 3D, collage, footage, UI, particles
- Composition, focal hierarchy, scale, negative space, and depth
- Palette, typography, materials, lighting, and texture
- Motion vocabulary, easing, anticipation, follow-through, and camera choreography
- Transition types and whether each is motivated
- On-screen text density and readability
- Voice, pace, pauses, music, effects, silence, and sound mix
- Information density, pacing, retention devices, and accessibility
- Techniques that could be recreated with deterministic code or open tools

## Output
1. Timestamped scene breakdown.
2. Craft observations and evidence, with uncertainty clearly marked.
3. Reusable abstract techniques, not copied shots.
4. Implementation mapping to Remotion, SVG, GSAP, Three.js, Blender, FFmpeg, or optional AI media tools.
5. A fresh treatment applying the techniques to a different topic.
6. QC risks and measurable acceptance criteria.

Do not infer exact software or settings from appearance alone. Do not reproduce copyrighted music, scripts, distinctive character designs, or a shot sequence shot-for-shot.
